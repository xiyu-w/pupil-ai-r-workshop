Repository: https://github.com/arvo2026-workshop/arvo2026-workshop

License source: https://github.com/arvo2026-workshop/arvo2026-workshop/blob/main/LICENSE

The optional classroom design lab adapts the **initial prompt/code → additional design context → evaluation → revised analysis with backup code** teaching pattern.
